Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · Internet Measured is an independent project based in Serbia

1. Use of YouTube API Services

Internet Measured uses YouTube API Services. By using anything we provide that relies on YouTube API Services, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What information we access

Public data. Our internal research tool reads publicly available information through the YouTube Data API: video IDs, titles, descriptions, publish dates, durations, categories, public view/like/comment counts, public channel statistics, and public most-popular charts by country.

Our own channel. The tool also reads analytics for our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Analytics API. This requires a one-time sign-in by the channel owner with Google (OAuth 2.0) and uses read-only permissions only (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly). No other person signs in to our tool.

We do not collect private information, contact details, email addresses, payment data or information about the people who watch videos.

3. How we use it

We analyse the data in aggregate to research online video trends and publish our conclusions as documentary videos. Figures we calculate ourselves are labelled as independent calculations, not YouTube metrics.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share stored API data with any internal or external party. Only our own team uses the research tool. Our published videos contain our conclusions and charts, not the raw stored data.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored on our own computer, not on a shared or public server. We keep stored API data only as long as the YouTube API Services Developer Policies permit for our approved use, and we refresh or delete it when the policies require.

6. Cookies and devices

This website does not use cookies, analytics, trackers or advertising, and does not store or read information on your device. No third parties serve content or place cookies through this website.

7. Revoking access and deleting data

The channel owner can revoke our tool's access to their Google account at any time through the Google security settings page. After revocation we delete the authorised data within the time required by YouTube's policies.

If you believe we store public data related to you or your channel and you want it deleted, email us at stanasko@gmail.com. We will delete it within 7 days.

8. Contact

Internet Measured · Serbia · stanasko@gmail.com

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
